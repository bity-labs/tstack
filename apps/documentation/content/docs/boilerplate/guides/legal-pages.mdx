The boilerplate includes public legal pages:

| Route | File |
| --- | --- |
| `/terms-of-service` | `src/app/(marketing)/terms-of-service/page.tsx` |
| `/privacy` | `src/app/(marketing)/privacy/page.tsx` |

## What to replace

Review every placeholder and product assumption before launch:

- company/legal entity name,
- app name,
- support and legal contact email,
- effective date,
- jurisdiction,
- product/service description,
- payment/refund policy,
- analytics and cookie disclosures,
- data retention and deletion language,
- third-party providers.

## Keep routes public

Legal pages are whitelisted in middleware and remain accessible in landing mode. If you rename them, update:

- `src/config/routes.ts`,
- `src/middleware.ts`,
- `src/components/footer.tsx`,
- page metadata in `src/locales/index.ts`.

## Legal warning

The included pages are generic templates. Have them reviewed by a qualified legal professional before production, especially if you process payments, handle sensitive data, or operate across jurisdictions.
